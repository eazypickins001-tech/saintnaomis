# 04 — CAPTIONS, HASHTAGS & PINNED COMMENTS

> Filled for Saint Naomis Foundation. Every post reuses the structure below and
> changes only the hook, the body and the CTA.

---

## 1. Caption rules

1. **The first line is the hook.** Same punch as the on-screen text, rewritten
   for the platform. Never start with context.
2. **Short lines.** One idea per line. Line breaks are pacing.
3. **Hashtags at the end** of the caption on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and
   LinkedIn. In the description on YouTube.
4. **The link goes in the bio** on TikTok and Instagram. Put the full link in
   the caption or description on Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn.
5. **One CTA.** Never "follow, like, share and comment".
6. **The caption must work if the video never plays.**
7. **No em dashes, no emojis, no exclamation marks.** Brand rule.
8. **Every number gets a source line.** `Source: Saint Naomis programme report,
   November 2026.` or the named public source.
9. **Every face gets the line** `Illustrative image. Not a real student.`
10. **Every composite gets the line** `Composite: one girl's route, built from
    our intake notes.`
11. **Every distress post gets** `This is not medical advice. If someone is in
    danger, contact local emergency services.`
12. **No exam, admission or scholarship promise.**
13. **CTA matches the live sitemap.** The live site lists five programs and
    cost labels. It does not list SAT, GED, IELTS or university admission
    assistance as services. Teach the test, do not sell a class. Point every
    CTA to `/programs/`, `/get-involved/#apply`, `/donate/`,
    `/stories-of-impact/`, `/kits-for-school/` or `/floortodesk/`.
14. **Use the live site's line** `Her Voice. Her Rights. Her Life.` on the end
    card and in at least one line of the caption when it fits.

## 2. Caption templates

### TikTok / Instagram Reels

```
[HOOK, verbatim from the video]

[One line of stakes or context.]

[One line of the mechanism or the reveal.]

[CTA]. Link in bio.

[Compliance line: composite, illustrative image, or not medical advice]

[8-12 hashtags]
```

### YouTube Shorts (searchable)

```
[HOOK] | [what the file actually explains, in search keywords]

[2-3 sentences expanding the mechanism. Name the exam, the step or the
programme. Include the real next step.]

Next step: https://saintnaomis.org/get-involved/

[8-12 hashtags]
```

### Facebook

```

[2-3 complete sentences. Facebook rewards reading. Include the place, the exam

[8-12 hashtags]
```

### X / Threads

## 3. Hashtag strategy

Use **8-12 per post** across the three tiers. Keep the core set stable; rotate
two or three reach tags per post.

```
REACH:
#GirlsEducation #Education #Nonprofit #Nigeria #Baltimore #StudyAbroad

AUDIENCE:
#Parent #Student #Teacher #Mentor #Donor #UniversityApplicant

NICHE:
#SAT #IELTS #GED #UniversityAdmissions #Scholarships #SDG4 #SDG5
#GirlChildEducation #ExamPrep #FutureYou

BRAND:
#SaintNaomis #SaintNaomisFoundation #HerVoiceHerRightsHerLife
```

**Rules:**

- Always include `#SaintNaomis`.
- Test-prep posts include the exam tag: `#SAT`, `#IELTS` or `#GED`.
- Admissions posts include `#UniversityAdmissions` or `#Scholarships`.
- Parent posts include `#Parent` and the city or country that is actually in
  the post.
- Never use a hashtag the content does not earn.
- No hashtag stuffing in the first line.

## 4. Pinned comments

Every post gets one pinned comment, written before publishing. Its job is to
start the thread, not to summarise the post.

Patterns that work for this channel:

- **The receipt:** `The programme report with these figures is linked in our
  bio. Check it before you take our word for it.`
- **The usefulness:** `If you are applying this year, the first step is at
  saintnaomis.org/get-involved.`
- **The stake:** `One missed deadline can cost a whole year. That is the part
  nobody explains.`
- **The invitation:** `Which file should we open next: the exam fee or the
  interview?`
- **The permission note:** `This is a composite story from our intake notes.
  No real student is shown.`
- **The real story:** `Rejoice said it in her own words: "Saint Naomis guided me
  to find myself and gain confidence. I have started a business, and now I have
  a direction." Full story in the bio.`
- **The correction:** `We got a detail wrong in this post. Here is the correct
  version.`

**Do not** pin "Thanks for watching" or anything that closes the conversation.

## 5. Comment seeding (three per post)

Each post ships with three comments in `03_POST_BANK.md`. They must be:

1. **Specific** to this post, not any post.
2. **Answerable** in five words.
3. **Non-promotional.** They invite, they do not sell.

Weak to strong:

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Great work" | "Which question made you feel supported instead of judged?" |
| "Thoughts?" | "What did your school ask for that your family could not buy?" |
| "Please donate" | "Which matters more for admission: the score or the essay?" |

Avoid: `Wow, this is so powerful!` · `This is amazing!` · `Very inspiring!` ·
`Great work!` · `Who agrees?`

---

## 6. Publishing rhythm

- 4 posts per week: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday.
- The strongest story post goes out on Sunday.
- One service CTA per eight posts. The rest end on a question, a save or a
  share.
- Never two asks back to back.
- Post, then pin the comment immediately.
- Reply to every comment for the first 90 days, as a person. Sign with a first
  name when the reply is personal.
