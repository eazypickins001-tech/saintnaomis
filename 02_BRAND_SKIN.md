# 02 — BRAND SKIN

> **Saint Naomis Foundation's own skin, built from the live logo.** The shared
> engine (hook architecture, 12 categories, prompt formula, post format) is
> identical to every repo built from this template. This skin must never be
> reused by another project.

---

## 0. The one law: be the opposite of the feed

**The expected look in this niche:** talking heads against flat colour, stock
photos of smiling schoolgirls, graduation caps, score cards, yellow sunbursts
and captions that announce a pledge total.

**Our opposite:** a warm, cinematic documentary still, one girl or one object,
available light, real rooms. The frame is calm. A thin soft-green file rule and
a small `SN / D07` tag sit in the corner. Three small white birds cross the
frame at the moment something changes. No graduation caps, no raised fists, no
logo sting longer than a second.

**Archetype:** Warm human. Named version: **The Green File**.

The "file" system is frame furniture, not a second archetype. The human story
is always the subject.

## 2. Palette (from the logo and the live site tokens)

The logo is a green globe with a woman's profile and three white birds, set
beside the `SaintNaomis` wordmark. Its greens run from a bright leaf green to a
deep forest green. The site tokens below are the approved production values.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | Clean white. 45% of every frame. |
| Soft band | `#EAF6E5` | File rule, label, end card. 15%. |
| Brand green | `#239400` | One underlined word, file accent, CTA button. 12%. |
| Dark green | `#1B7300` | Small text, links, pressed stamp. 5%. |
| Logo deep green | `#104C02` | Shadow, end card, logo matte. 3%. |
| Ink | `#1F2A1F` | Body text, shadows. 5%. |
| Natural tones | warm brown skin, navy uniform, wood, chalk | The photograph. 15%. |
| Accent yellow | `#FFC93C` | One number or badge per post only. Never a background. |

**Ratio rule:** 45% white · 15% soft green · 15% natural tones · 12% brand green
· 8% ink and dark green · 5% accent. Never flood the frame with green.

**Contrast rule (from the site brief):** white text on `#239400` is about 4:1.
Use it only at 24 px or larger, or 18.66 px or larger in bold. Small green text
on white uses `#1B7300`.

**Logo rule:** the globe and profile mark is the avatar. Never crop the birds
out of the avatar. Never place the logo over a face. Never animate the logo for
more than 0.8 s.

## 4. Five fingerprints (named)

1. **The Three Birds.** White, small, always three. They lift at the turn, never
   at the hook. They are a promise that something is about to change.
2. **The SN File Tag.** `SN / D07` bottom-left, Courier Prime, soft-green tape.
   Built in the editor, never generated.
3. **The Soft-Green Rule.** A thin `#EAF6E5` line across the lower third. It is
   the frame's spine and the place captions sit.
4. **The Bird-Lift Wipe.** Three birds cross left to right to hide a cut. One
   wipe per post maximum.
5. **The One Underlined Word.** Each caption underlines exactly one word in
   `#239400`. The underline lands on the spoken stress.

## 5. Motion grammar

| Beat | Time | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| Signal / open | 0.00–0.10 s | White or first frame of the subject |
| Globe mark | 0.10–0.40 s | Logo globe and profile, matte on white |
| Bird lift | 0.40–0.55 s | Three birds rise from the mark |
| Wordmark | 0.55–0.80 s | `SaintNaomis` lands, one soft pop |
| Snap out | 0.80–1.00 s | Hard cut to Clip 1 |
| The footage | 1.0 s+ | One subject, one action, available light |
| File label | 1.0–2.5 s | `SN / D07` and the file name, taped in |
| Captions | 2.5 s+ | Bottom third, six words max |
| The turn | 16–32 s | Three birds cross the frame |
| Sign-off | last 2.4 s | Soft-green end card, logo, one CTA |

**Intro (0.8 s):** white, globe mark, three birds lift, wordmark, hard cut.
Three elements maximum. No whoosh over 0.2 s.

**Outro (2.4 s):** 0.0–0.4 s last content frame holds; 0.4–1.6 s soft-green end
card with the logo, the line `Her Voice. Her Rights. Her Life.` and one CTA;
1.6–2.4 s hold. Audio: two warm notes, then silence.

**Loop rule:** if the post loops, the last frame is the white or soft-green card
that opens the intro. Never force a loop.

## 6. Voice

- **Who speaks:** The Teacher. Warm, low, unhurried, a little dry. Never
  breathless, never sorrowful, never a preacher.
- **Person:** "you" for the viewer, "she" for a girl, "we" only when the
  Foundation is actually speaking.
- **Sentence length:** short. One idea per sentence. If you run out of breath,
  cut it.
- **Forbidden:** em dashes, emojis, exclamation marks, "empowering",
  "underserved" as a noun, "the less fortunate", "give a voice", "change a
  life", "every girl deserves", graduation-cap imagery, raised fists.
- **Sign-off (spoken, same every post):** `Keep her in the room.`
- **End-card brand line:** `Her Voice. Her Rights. Her Life.` This is the live
  site's line. It sits under the logo; it is not a second CTA.
- **Source line:** every post that carries a number or a service ends its
  caption with `Source: [programme report / named public source], [month year].`
- **AI-face line:** every post that shows a face ends its caption with
  `Illustrative image. Not a real student.`

## 10. Safeguarding and AI-face rule

1. Every character is a composite, played by an AI-generated actor. No real
   student is depicted.
2. Any post that shows a face carries `Illustrative image. Not a real student.`
   in the caption.
3. No image of a child crying, undressed, injured or in distress. Use absence,
   the object, the empty room, the back, the hands.
4. No diagnosis, no clinical claim, no legal claim. Distress posts describe
   noticing and support only.
5. A named real student appears only with written parent or guardian consent,
   and only in her own words.

## 12. Anti-patterns

- Graduation caps, raised fists, yellow sunbursts, stock "African child"
  photography, "empowering" hand-lettering.
- More than five colours, or green used as a full-frame background.
- A corporate template with a long logo sting.
- A face of a real student, or an AI face presented as a real student.
- A score card, acceptance letter or certificate generated by AI.
- Changing the skin because one post underperformed. Change the post.
