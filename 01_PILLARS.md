Rules:

- Every post belongs to exactly one pillar.
- Never two consecutive posts in the same pillar, unless the sub-type differs.
- The share column is a target, not a quota. The standing instruction wins.
- **Named-series exception:** Global Education runs as a named series (the
  Application Room, Test Myth, One Door, 60 Seconds Before Your Exam). Within
  the series the sub-type differs every post, so the feed does not read as one
  topic.
- **Service boundary:** Global Education is a content pillar, not a confirmed
  service. The live site does not list SAT, GED, IELTS or admission assistance.
  Posts in this pillar teach a test or an admissions step. They do not advertise
  a service. CTAs point to the five live programs at `/programs/`.

## 3. Rotation rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| No two consecutive posts in the same INTERRUPT category | The audience adapts to a repeated trick within days |
| No two consecutive posts in the same pillar, unless the sub-type differs | Prevents the feed reading as one topic |
| At least 4 different categories per week | Keeps the catalogue visually varied |
| One Global Education post per week outside a named series | Protects trust; not every post is an ad for a test |
| One Transformation Story per ~8 posts | Prevents miracle framing |
| Never two asks in a row | The feed should not feel like a sales page |
| No exam result, admission or scholarship promise, ever | Compliance and trust |
