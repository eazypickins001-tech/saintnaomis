SAINT NAOMIS FOUNDATION
30-DAY SHORT-FORM AI VIDEO CONTENT PLAN
Production format: 40-60 second videos
Primary generation tools: Google Omni (up to 10 sec/clip) and Veo (up to 8 sec/clip)
Audience: African girls/women, parents, students, university applicants and education-focused communities

PRODUCTION RULES
================
1. Every human character is Black/African in complexion unless a concept explicitly requires otherwise.
2. Each generated clip stays within the model limit:
   - Omni: maximum 10 seconds per generated clip.
   - Veo: maximum 8 seconds per generated clip.
3. Each finished video is 40-60 seconds.
4. Storyboards use clip-level timing, not only broad scene timing.
5. Character continuity must be specified whenever a character returns: age range, complexion, hairstyle, wardrobe and setting.
6. The first 1-2 seconds must contain a visual or narrative pattern interrupt.
7. Pattern interrupts are purposeful, not decoration. Use gravity/physics, scale, time, glitch, material swaps, mirrors, impossible objects, anatomy, camera perspective, emotion/expectation subversion, light/color and typography.
8. Narration should be conversational and emotionally precise.
9. Avoid generic NGO language such as "empowering communities" unless it is earned by a specific story.
10. Every video should create at least one of these reactions: "Wait, what?", "That could be me", "I never thought about it that way", "Is this true?", "I need to send this to someone."
11. CTAs should be soft and context-specific. Do not sell in every video.
12. Educational service content should feel like opportunity content first and advertising second.

CONTENT PILLARS
================
A. The Hidden Story: girls, women, safety, education, trauma and social pressure.
B. The Future She Could Have: aspiration, confidence, mentorship and transformation.
C. Hard Truths: uncomfortable misconceptions and overlooked problems.
D. Parent Radar: things parents should notice, ask or understand.
E. Student Advantage: study habits, exam preparation, confidence and opportunity.
F. Global Education: SAT, GED, IELTS and university admission assistance.
G. Transformation Stories: before/after journeys without exploiting trauma.
H. Community Conversations: questions designed to generate real comments and stories.

COMMENT STRATEGY
================
Do not post comments that sound like brand employees praising their own content.
Rotate:
- Personal memory questions
- "Would you..." dilemmas
- Parent/student disagreements
- Experience questions
- Tagging prompts
- Contrarian questions
- Specific educational questions
- "What happened next?" curiosity prompts

Avoid:
"Wow, this is so powerful!"
"This is amazing!"
"Very inspiring!"
"Great work!"
"Who agrees?"

VISUAL CONTINUITY MASTER RULE
=============================
Whenever the same character returns, repeat the exact character descriptor in the generation prompt. Example:

"15-year-old Black African girl, dark brown complexion, natural shoulder-length twists, expressive brown eyes, white school blouse, navy skirt, black school shoes."

Do not change age, complexion, hairstyle or wardrobe between clips unless the story explicitly shows a time jump.

GENERAL AI VIDEO NEGATIVE GUIDANCE
==================================
Avoid: non-African human characters, changing faces, changing hairstyles, changing clothing without narrative reason, extra fingers, distorted hands, duplicate people, floating facial features, unreadable text, logos, watermarks, random subtitles, camera drift, unexplained scene changes, cartoon appearance, excessive cinematic effects that compete with the story.

For typography-based clips, generate clean space for post-production text whenever the video model struggles to render exact words. Text can be added in editing/GHL workflow rather than relying on the model.
