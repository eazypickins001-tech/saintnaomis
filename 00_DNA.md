## 3. The mechanics everyone in this niche shares

1. One subject, one thesis. Never a listicle.
2. A specific moment reads as truth; a general claim reads as marketing.
3. An injustice engine: a girl, an avoidable exit, a system that let it happen.
4. Authority through specificity: place names, exam names, deadlines, programme
   names.
5. Identity binding: the viewer is a parent, a student, a mentor, a donor or a
   girl who nearly left.
6. An open loop in the first three seconds, closed before the CTA.
7. Serialisation: named series and numbered files so the catalogue compounds.
8. A second engine behind the channel: applications, mentoring, donations.
9. Absence over depiction when the truth would exploit a real person.

**Ours:**

1. One girl, one moment, one decision per post.
2. A specific exam, form, deadline or room, never "the education system".
3. The avoidable moment, not the rescue.
4. Real programme names and real next steps, never vague "impact".
5. The viewer is addressed by role within 1.5 seconds.
6. The loop is a physical question: what is she carrying that nobody sees?
7. Every post is a numbered file in a named series.
8. The CTA is a soft next step: follow, save, send, ask, apply, mentor.
9. No real student's story without permission. No diagnosis. No fake result.

## 5. Format spec (do not deviate)

- **Length:** 48 s (6 × 8 s) for this bank. The window is 40-60 s.
- **Cadence:** 4 posts per week. One story post, three shorter social cuts.
- **Clip limit:** Veo 8 s, Omni 10 s. Never generate a longer clip.
- **Structure:**
  - `HOOK` (0-8 s) — the interrupt, no logo, no greeting
  - `SETUP` (8-16 s) — identity, stakes
  - `TURN` (16-24 s) — the mechanism or the contradiction
  - `TURN 2` (24-32 s) — escalation, the second cost
  - `PAYOFF` (32-40 s) — deliver the promise
  - `CLOSE + CTA` (40-48 s) — meaning, then one line
- **Visual grammar:** AI for metaphor, memory, atmosphere and diagrams. Real
  screenshots for results, records and testimonials. Never AI for a real
  student, a real score, an acceptance letter or a result.
- **Text:** always in the editor, never from the model. This includes scores,
  labels, doors, signs, notifications and report cards.
- **Continuity:** every returning character gets the exact continuity clause
  pasted into every clip. Do not change age, complexion, hairstyle or wardrobe
  without a stated time jump.
- **Series:** every post is a numbered file. Asset names follow
  `SN_D01_EmptyChair`.

## 7. Integrity guardrails (project-specific additions)

The universal five are in `_SHARED/prompt-formula.md` §7. Add these:

1. **No real student is played by AI.** Every character is a composite,
   performed by an AI-generated actor. Captions that show a face carry
   `Illustrative image. Not a real student.`
2. **No fake result.** Never generate a score, an acceptance letter, a
   certificate or an analytics screenshot. Real records only, or none.
3. **No diagnosis.** Posts about distress, silence or invisible girls describe
   noticing and support. They do not diagnose, treat or promise recovery. Add
   `This is not medical advice` where the subject is distress.
4. **No service claim without confirmation.** SAT, GED, IELTS and university
   admission assistance are in the approved content plan. Confirm each is live
   before publishing an offer. Otherwise use "Ask what support is available."
5. **No outcome promise.** No "guaranteed admission", no "pass IELTS", no
   "you will get a scholarship".
6. **African characters by default.** Every human is Black or African in
   complexion unless the concept explicitly requires otherwise. Negative prompts
   must exclude non-African characters and identity drift.
7. **No poverty porn.** No torn clothes, no crying child, no rescue framing, no
   saviour narrative.

## 8. What this channel will never do

1. Show a real student's face or story without written permission.
2. Invent a statistic, a testimony, a score, an acceptance letter or a partner.
3. Use graduation caps, raised fists, stock "African child" photography or
   generic "empowering communities" copy.
4. Promise an exam result, an admission or a scholarship.
5. Diagnose a mental health condition or give clinical or legal advice.
6. Use an em dash in published copy, or an emoji anywhere.
